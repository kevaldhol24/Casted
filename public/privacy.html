Casted — Privacy

Last updated 9 October 2026

Casted collects anonymous gameplay data to find out which puzzles are too hard, too easy or confusing, so we can improve them. We never collect your name, email, account, contacts, precise location or anything else that identifies you, and we don't use cookies.

What we collect

Data Example
Gameplay events Level started, solved or left; time taken; how close the shadow got to the target over time; hints used; pauses; which video offers were shown or chosen
Device details Device type (phone, tablet, computer), operating system, browser name, screen size, pixel density, graphics quality setting, language, time-zone offset
Random ids A random player id kept in your browser's local storage, and a new random id for each visit. They link your sessions together but say nothing about who you are
Game version Which build of the game you played

What we don't collect

  • No IP address is stored. Our server never reads or saves it, and its request logs are switched off.
  • No personal data, no advertising ids, no cookies, no tracking across other sites.
  • We don't sell or share this data. It is only used to improve the game.

How long we keep it

Gameplay records are deleted automatically 12 months after they are received.

Your choices

  • Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes the random player id; the game then starts with a new one.
  • Your game progress is saved in your browser (on CrazyGames, signed-in players' progress is kept by CrazyGames so it follows them across devices), never on our server.

Playing on a game portal

When you play Casted on a game portal such as CrazyGames, the portal runs its own ads and analytics under its own privacy policy. Please read the portal's policy for that part.

Where the data is stored

Records are stored in a Cloudflare database (Cloudflare D1) used only for this game.
